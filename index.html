Design Approach & Journey

I am a Digital Game Design undergraduate at Kahramanmaraş Sütçü İmam University (2024–2028) based in Kemer, Antalya. My work centers on core gameplay loops, systems design, game economy balancing, and responsive player feedback.

Across Unity, C#, and GameMaker, I have designed and prototyped six games spanning roguelite dungeon crawlers, shop management sims, platformers, and word puzzles. During my IT internship at Kemer Municipality, I designed and built Kelime Kültür Kemer to promote local culture and tourism through mobile gameplay.
